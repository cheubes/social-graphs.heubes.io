Catherine de' Medici's favourite son, he distinguished himself as a military commander at just eighteen, winning the battles of Jarnac and Moncontour against the Protestants in 1569. Elected King of Poland in 1573, he fled Kraków the following year on hearing of his brother Charles IX's death to claim the French throne. Married to Louise of Lorraine in 1575, he ruled a kingdom torn between the Catholic League and the Protestants, and had Henri de Guise and the Cardinal of Lorraine assassinated at the Château de Blois in December 1588 to reassert his authority. Allied with Henri de Navarre against the League, he was assassinated by the monk Jacques Clément in August 1589, the last king of the Valois dynasty.
