The daughter of Holy Roman Emperor Maximilian II, this Habsburg archduchess married Charles IX in 1570 in a match that strengthened ties between France and Austria in the midst of the Wars of Religion.

Queen of France, noted for her gentleness and piety, she remained largely apart from political affairs and bore a single daughter, who died in infancy.

After Charles IX's death in 1574, she declined several remarriage proposals and returned to Vienna, where she founded a convent and devoted herself to religious life until her death in 1592.
