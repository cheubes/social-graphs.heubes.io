The second daughter of Catherine de' Medici, she married Charles III of Lorraine in 1559 at the age of eleven, strengthening ties between the French crown and the Duchy of Lorraine.

Described by contemporaries as notably discreet and retiring, unlike several of her siblings, she led a quiet court life in Nancy.

The mother of nine children, including the future Duke Henry II of Lorraine, she died in childbirth in 1575 at the age of twenty-seven.
