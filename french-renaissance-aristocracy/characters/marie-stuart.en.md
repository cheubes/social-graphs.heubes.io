Made Queen of Scots at six days old, she was sent to the French court at the age of five, where she grew up under the guardianship of her grandmother Antoinette de Bourbon and her Guise uncles. She married the Dauphin Francis, later Francis II, in 1558 and became Queen of France, only to be widowed in 1560 when her young husband died. Back in Scotland, a series of scandals, including the murder of her second husband, forced her to abdicate in 1567 and flee to England to seek the protection of her cousin Elizabeth I. Regarded as a threat to the English throne, she was imprisoned there for nearly nineteen years before being executed in 1587.
