The wife, from 1513, of Claude of Lorraine, first Duke of Guise, she bore about a dozen children and saw several of them, including François de Guise and the Cardinal of Lorraine, rise to the highest offices of the kingdom. Grandmother of Mary, Queen of Scots through her daughter Marie de Guise, she oversaw her family's interests and marriage alliances with lasting authority. The matriarch of her house, she died in 1583 at almost eighty-nine, having witnessed both the rise and the first setbacks of the House of Guise during the Wars of Religion.
