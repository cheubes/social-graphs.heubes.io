The eldest daughter of Henry II and Catherine de' Medici, she was first betrothed to Philip II's son, the Infante Don Carlos, before the terms of the Treaty of Cateau-Cambrésis were revised so that she would marry the Spanish king himself in 1559.

As Philip II's third wife and Queen consort of Spain, she bore him two daughters and is remembered as his most beloved wife.

She died in Madrid in 1568, aged twenty-three, following a miscarriage.
