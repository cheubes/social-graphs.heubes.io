Born on 2 April 1546 at Fontainebleau, the eldest daughter of Henry II and Catherine de' Medici, she grows up at court alongside Mary, Queen of Scots, and is first betrothed to Philip II's son, the Infante Don Carlos, before the terms of the Treaty of Cateau-Cambrésis of 1559 are revised so that she would marry the Spanish king himself; the proxy wedding takes place on 22 June 1559, but she does not meet Philip II until 31 January 1560, who reportedly quips, seeing her stare at him: "Are you checking whether I have white hair?"

Married at thirteen, the union is not consummated until May 1561, at fifteen. As Philip II's third wife and Queen consort of Spain, Naples and Sicily, Duchess of Burgundy and Milan among other titles, she bears him two surviving daughters, Isabella Clara Eugenia in 1566 and Catherine Michelle in 1567, after losing twins in 1564, and is remembered as his most beloved wife.

She dies in Madrid on 3 October 1568, aged twenty-two, following a miscarriage, six months pregnant with a daughter. Her elder daughter is later considered a claimant to the French throne.
