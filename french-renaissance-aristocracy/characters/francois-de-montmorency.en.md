The eldest son of Anne de Montmorency, Marshal of France and Governor of Paris, he pursued, unlike many members of the high Catholic nobility, a policy of religious moderation during the Wars of Religion.

Close to the moderate Politiques faction that his brother Henri de Montmorency-Damville also joined, he was for a time suspected of complicity with the Malcontents conspiracy and imprisoned on Catherine de' Medici's orders in 1574.

Released some time later, he died in 1579 without ever fully regaining the influence he had held before his disgrace.
