Henri II's favourite for over twenty years, she wielded considerable influence over the kingdom's affairs and access to the king. Upon Henri II's death in 1559, her long-time rival Catherine de' Medici forced her to exchange the Château de Chenonceau for the less prestigious Château de Chaumont.
