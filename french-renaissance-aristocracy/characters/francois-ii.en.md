The eldest son of Catherine de' Medici and Henry II, he married Mary, Queen of Scots in 1558 before becoming King of France the following year after his father's accidental death. Frail since childhood, he never truly governed: power fell to his young wife's uncles, François de Guise and the Cardinal of Lorraine, whose dominance provoked the Amboise conspiracy in March 1560. His reign, dominated by the House of Guise, lasted less than eighteen months and ended with his premature death from an ear infection, leaving the throne to his younger brother Charles IX.
