The youngest of Catherine de' Medici's children, Duke of Alençon and later Duke of Anjou, he led the Malcontents faction against his brother Henri III during the Wars of Religion. His death in 1584, leaving Henri III without an heir, opened the way for Henri of Navarre and precipitated the formation of the Catholic League.
