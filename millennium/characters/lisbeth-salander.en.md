A private investigator for Milton Security and an exceptional hacker, able to break into the most secure systems under the handle Wasp.

A taciturn, fiercely independent young woman, she carries the scars of a childhood marked by her father's violence and by a psychiatric and judicial system that wrongly declared her incompetent. Placed under legal guardianship despite her extraordinary intelligence, she methodically takes revenge on those who abuse their power over her, starting with her guardian Nils Bjurman.

Unknowingly recruited by Mikael Blomkvist for the investigation into Harriet Vanger's disappearance, she becomes his recurring partner, before the revelation of her own past, as Zalachenko's daughter, pulls her back to the centre of the story through to the trial that closes the trilogy.
