A lawyer appointed as Lisbeth Salander's new legal guardian, he abuses the authority this position gives him to sexually assault her, before she takes her revenge and holds a lasting grip over him in turn. Present alongside Gunnar Björck at Zalachenko's defection years earlier, he later did occasional work for the Section.
