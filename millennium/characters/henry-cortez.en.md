The youngest journalist on the Millénium staff, ambitious and full of energy, he cuts his teeth under Mikael Blomkvist's wing. He takes an increasingly active role in the magazine's investigations alongside Malin Eriksson, notably in picking up Dag Svensson's trafficking investigation and later in covering the Salander affair.
