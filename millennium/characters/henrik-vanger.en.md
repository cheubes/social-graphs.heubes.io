An octogenarian patriarch, retired from running the Vanger industrial group but still its most influential shareholder despite the rivalries tearing his family apart. Every year, on the anniversary of Harriet's disappearance, he receives a pressed, framed flower, just like the ones she used to give him as a child, a macabre ritual he spends decades reading as a taunt from her presumed killer. Consumed by this obsession, he devotes his fortune and the final years of his life to getting to the bottom of her disappearance, and hires Mikael Blomkvist, under cover of writing a family biography, to pick up the investigation where the police left off. Clear-eyed about the dark corners of his own family, including his brother Harald and his great-nephew Martin, he sees his suspicions confirmed far beyond what he had feared, before living to witness his reunion with Harriet.
