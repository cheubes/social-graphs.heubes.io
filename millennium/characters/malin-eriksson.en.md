Managing editor at Millénium, hired after working as a freelancer, she quickly establishes herself as an essential part of the newsroom through her diligence and organisational skill. A single mother to a young son, she keeps the magazine running day to day and takes over as acting editor-in-chief when Erika Berger is away, steering the publication of Dag Svensson's investigation with a level head after his murder.
