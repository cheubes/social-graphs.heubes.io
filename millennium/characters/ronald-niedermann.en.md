Lisbeth Salander's half-brother, born in Germany from an earlier liaison of Zalachenko's, a wrestler-built giant afflicted with a congenital insensitivity to pain, a condition he himself experiences as a curse. A devoted but fearful enforcer for his father, he carries out the network's dirty work, kidnappings and executions, with apparent indifference, going as far as murdering Dag Svensson and Mia Bergman to protect Zalachenko's secret. After the shootout at Gosseberga, where he leaves his dying father behind, he goes on the run across Sweden, leaving a trail of victims, before finally being caught up by his past within organised crime.
