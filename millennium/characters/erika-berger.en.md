Editor-in-chief and co-founder of Millénium, Mikael Blomkvist's partner since the magazine's earliest days, with whom she has had a long-standing affair, known to and accepted by her husband Greger Beckman. A strong-willed and shrewd manager, she keeps the magazine afloat through difficult times, not least when Mikael's libel conviction threatens its credibility. She temporarily leaves Millénium in the third book to take over as editor-in-chief of a major national daily, the Svenska Morgon-Posten, where she has to contend with an anonymous harassment campaign, before returning to run the magazine.
