Art director at Millénium since its founding, responsible for the magazine's layout and visual identity, and a close friend of Mikael Blomkvist and Erika Berger. Openly gay and in a stable relationship, he brings the newsroom a steadiness and polish that contrast with the turmoil of Mikael's investigations. A shareholder in the magazine alongside Erika and Mikael, he takes an active part in decisions that shape Millénium's future, particularly when it must weather the media and legal storm stirred up by the Salander affair.
