Henrik Vanger's great-niece, an introverted, devout teenager, scarred by the abuse inflicted on her by her father Gottfried and, she fears, by her cousin Martin.

She vanishes at sixteen during a family gathering on the Vanger island in 1966, without a body or an explanation, a mystery that haunts Henrik for thirty-six years and forms the heart of the first book's plot.

In reality, she staged her own disappearance with her cousin Anita's help, fleeing both her family's grip and the guilt of having let her father die without going to his aid. Found in Australia, married under the name Cochran, she agrees to reconnect with Henrik and reclaim, in part, her place within the Vanger family.
