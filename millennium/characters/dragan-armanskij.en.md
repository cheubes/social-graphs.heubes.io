Director of Milton Security, a well-regarded private security firm where he hired Lisbeth Salander despite the misgivings of those around him, won over by her extraordinary abilities. Of Croatian and Armenian descent, a pragmatic and loyal man, he feels a protective affection for his young employee that he sometimes struggles to explain even to himself, torn between exasperation at her temperament and admiration for her talent. When Lisbeth is wrongly accused of murder and later targeted by her father's network, he does not hesitate to put his agency's resources and staff behind protecting her and uncovering the truth, alongside Mikael Blomkvist.
