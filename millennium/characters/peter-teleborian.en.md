A respected psychiatrist, director of the Sankt Stefan clinic where he had Lisbeth Salander forcibly institutionalised at the age of twelve, acting on the Section's orders rather than any genuine diagnosis, and where he subjected her to prolonged restraint that leaves a lasting mark on her. A court-appointed expert with a veneer of respectability, he conceals behind his medical authority paedophilic impulses he never allows himself to act on openly. Contacted again by the Section when Lisbeth resurfaces in the news, he agrees to draft a new, entirely fabricated psychiatric report to have her institutionalised once more, before his expertise is publicly demolished at the trial in the third book by a witness with access to his file.
