A former Soviet military intelligence (GRU) officer, he defects to Sweden in the 1970s and, under the cover identity of Karl Axel Bodin, becomes one of the most valuable informants of Sweden's Cold War, protected as such for decades by a covert faction within the Säpo, the Section. Father of Lisbeth and Camilla Salander, he abuses their mother Agneta for years, until Lisbeth, as a teenager, tries to kill him by dousing him in petrol, an act for which she is institutionalised while he faces no consequences. Settled in Sweden under legal cover, he builds a sprawling criminal network, prostitution and arms trafficking, beyond the law's reach, with Ronald Niedermann carrying out the dirty work. Tracked down and attacked again by Lisbeth in 2005, he survives mutilated, only to be shot dead in his hospital bed by a former Section agent sent to silence what his trial threatened to expose.
