Harriet's cousin and CEO of the Vanger group, an outwardly composed and respected businessman, married but without children.

As a child he witnessed, if not took part in, his father Gottfried's crimes, and secretly inherited his impulses; Mikael Blomkvist's investigation reveals that he went on alone, for decades, murdering women in killings disguised as accidents or disappearances.

When Mikael uncovers his secret, Martin tries to kill him in turn in the lair he has fitted out beneath his house, before taking his own life in a car crash on the motorway to escape the police.
