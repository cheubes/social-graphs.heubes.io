An investigative journalist and co-founder of the magazine Millénium, known for his integrity and doggedness as much as for his discreet affairs. Convicted of libel after wrongly accusing financier Hans-Erik Wennerström, he puts his career on hold to investigate, at Henrik Vanger's request, the disappearance of his great-niece Harriet thirty-six years earlier. The investigation leads him to Lisbeth Salander, with whom he forms a recurring partnership, and to uncovering the Vanger family's serial killings. His collaboration with Lisbeth continues through the next two books, where he puts Millénium's weight and his own investigative instincts behind her defence, against the network that orchestrated her institutionalisation and later her trial.
