A retired lawyer, Lisbeth Salander's legal guardian from her release from a psychiatric hospital until a stroke forces him to step down, replaced by Nils Bjurman.

A kindly father figure and one of the very few adults Lisbeth has ever trusted, he continues to watch over her from his care home despite significant physical after-effects.

His long-standing knowledge of Lisbeth's case and his testimony prove decisive when Mikael Blomkvist and Annika Giannini set out to clear her name.
