A Jordan College kitchen boy and Lyra's inseparable partner in mischief, the two of them running half-wild through Oxford's rooftops and streets. His abduction by the Gobblers is the event that sets Lyra's journey North in motion, as she refuses to accept his disappearance and sets out to find him at any cost. Their reunion, and its aftermath, form the emotional core of the novel's ending.
