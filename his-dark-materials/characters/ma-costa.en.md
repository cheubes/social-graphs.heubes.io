A powerful, imposing gyptian matriarch of the Costa family, feared and respected among her people. She nursed Lyra as a baby during a period spent at Jordan College, a bond she has never forgotten. When Lyra later flees to the gyptian boats, Ma Costa shelters her again and reveals the first fragments of the truth about her parentage.
