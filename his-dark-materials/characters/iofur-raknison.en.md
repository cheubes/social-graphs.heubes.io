The usurper King of the armoured bears of Svalbard, who seized the throne through cunning rather than combat. Obsessed with becoming more human, he covets a dæmon of his own and surrounds himself with human customs and architecture, a weakness Lyra exploits to provoke him into a duel. He is ultimately killed by Iorek Byrnison, who reclaims the throne that was rightfully his.
