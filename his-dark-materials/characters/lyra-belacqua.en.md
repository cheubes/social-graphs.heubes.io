An eleven-year-old girl raised half-wild among the Scholars of Jordan College, mischievous and fiercely loyal, known also as Lyra Silvertongue for her talent for tall tales. When her friend Roger is abducted by the Gobblers, she sets out from Oxford to the Arctic North to rescue him, guided along the way by the alethiometer entrusted to her by the Master. Her journey leads her to uncover the truth about her own parentage, the nature of Dust, and the terrible purpose behind the General Oblation Board's experiments.
