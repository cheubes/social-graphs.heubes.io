A laconic, resourceful Texan aeronaut, hired by John Faa to fly the gyptian expedition North in his hot-air balloon. He and Iorek Byrnison fought together in an earlier war against the Tartars and remain close friends. Dry-witted and unflappable in a crisis, he quickly becomes one of Lyra's most steadfast companions on the journey.
