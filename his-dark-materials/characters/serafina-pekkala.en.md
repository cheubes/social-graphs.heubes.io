Queen of a witch clan from Lake Enara, centuries old yet outwardly youthful, as is the way of witches.

Decades before the novel, she and Farder Coram fell in love, though their union could never last given how briefly humans live compared to witches.

When Lyra's quest brings her North, Serafina Pekkala and her clan come to the aid of the gyptian expedition, providing crucial support in the assault on Bolvangar.
