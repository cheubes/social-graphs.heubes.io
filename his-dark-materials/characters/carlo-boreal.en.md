A refined, faintly unsettling lord who moves in the same circles as Mrs Coulter and the General Oblation Board. Lyra meets him at one of Mrs Coulter's London gatherings, where his serpent dæmon and quiet menace leave Pantalaimon deeply wary of him. In Will Parry's world, he operates under the alias Sir Charles Latrom, a double life that grows far more significant in the books that follow.
