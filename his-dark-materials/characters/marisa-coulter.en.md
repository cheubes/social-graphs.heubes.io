A charming, elegant woman whose warmth conceals a ruthless ambition.

She recruits Lyra as her assistant in London under the guise of mentorship, only for Lyra to discover that she heads the General Oblation Board, the organisation behind the Gobblers and the intercision experiments carried out at Bolvangar.

Her true nature is compounded by the revelation that she is Lyra's mother, a fact she and Lord Asriel had both concealed.
