A commanding, ambitious explorer and experimental theologian, and Lyra's father, though this is kept from her for most of the novel. His expeditions to the North uncover evidence of Dust and of a bridge to other worlds, a discovery he presents to the Scholars of Jordan College in a bid for funding, at the cost of nearly being poisoned by the Master. His relentless pursuit of this research, whatever the human cost, drives the novel toward its climax.
