King of the western gyptians, a huge, imposing man tempered by age into a shrewd and measured leader.

When the Gobblers' abductions of gyptian and Oxford children can no longer be ignored, he convenes the great council, the byanroping, and leads the rescue expedition North himself.

He formally takes Lyra into his care for the journey, trusting Farder Coram's judgment that she has a crucial part to play.
