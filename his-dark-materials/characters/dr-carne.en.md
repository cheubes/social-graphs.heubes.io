The Master of Jordan College, an old and grave scholar who has been Lyra's de facto guardian since her infancy, when Lord Asriel entrusted her to his care. Torn between his knowledge of the dangers awaiting her and his oath of secrecy, he goes as far as attempting, unsuccessfully, to poison Lord Asriel to keep him from involving Lyra in his research. Before she leaves Oxford for the North, he gives her the college's alethiometer and a warning she does not yet understand.
