An exiled prince of the armoured bears of Svalbard, stripped of his armour and his rank after a fight, and reduced to drunken servitude as a blacksmith in Trollesund.

Lyra helps him recover his stolen armour, restoring his fighting spirit, and the two become close allies on the journey North.

He ultimately reclaims his throne from the usurper Iofur Raknison in single combat, and remains one of Lyra's staunchest protectors.
