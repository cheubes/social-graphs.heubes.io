A wise, elderly gyptian, his body weakened by a witch's curse but his mind as sharp as ever, and John Faa's closest and most trusted advisor. Decades earlier he loved the witch queen Serafina Pekkala, a bond that still colours his judgement and his knowledge of witch affairs. He takes a particular interest in Lyra and her alethiometer, teaching her what he can of the art of reading it and treating her almost as a granddaughter.
