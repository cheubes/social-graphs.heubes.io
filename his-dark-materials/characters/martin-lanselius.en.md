The witches' consul at Trollesund, a human who has earned the trust of the witch clans through years of diplomatic service. When Farder Coram and Lyra come to him for help, he reveals that the witches have long known of a prophecy concerning a child destined to play a decisive role in the fate of Dust itself, and that Lyra fits its description. He also points them toward Iorek Byrnison's confiscated armour, giving the gyptian expedition a crucial ally for the journey North.
