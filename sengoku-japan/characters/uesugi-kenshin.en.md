A devout Buddhist monk of the war god Bishamonten, Uesugi Kenshin ruled Echigo Province and established himself as one of the most respected tacticians of his time, even in the eyes of his enemies.

His rivalry of more than a decade with Takeda Shingen, marked by the five battles of Kawanakajima, made for one of the most celebrated duels of feudal Japan, with neither side ever winning decisively. Having no biological children, he adopted his nephew Kagekatsu as heir; his sudden death in 1578, as he was preparing a major campaign against Oda Nobunaga, plunged the clan into a brief succession war and permanently removed one of the last serious threats to Oda hegemony.
