Another cousin and long-time companion-in-arms of Toyotomi Hideyoshi, Fukushima Masanori distinguished himself through battlefield bravery across the regent's major campaigns, from the siege of Odawara to the invasions of Korea, becoming one of the clan's most decorated generals.

Like Kato Kiyomasa, his rivalry with the administrator Ishida Mitsunari led him, after Hideyoshi's death, to side with Tokugawa Ieyasu. He played a leading role at the Battle of Sekigahara in 1600, where his troops opened hostilities against the Western army, directly contributing to Ieyasu's victory, whom he would go on to serve faithfully, though his loyalty always remained tinged with attachment to the old Toyotomi house.
