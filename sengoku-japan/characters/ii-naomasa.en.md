Taken in as a child by Tokugawa Ieyasu after his father's assassination, Ii Naomasa became one of his most flamboyant generals, commanding a unit entirely equipped in bright red armour and banners nicknamed the 'Red Devils,' whose mere sight on a battlefield sometimes sufficed to unnerve the enemy.

One of the Tokugawa's 'Four Guardian Kings,' he played a central role at the Battle of Sekigahara in 1600, where he was wounded, and afterward led the negotiations that won several former Western lords over to the new regime. He died two years later from complications of that wound.
