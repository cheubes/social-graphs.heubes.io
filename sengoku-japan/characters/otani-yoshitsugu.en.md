A capable administrator in Toyotomi Hideyoshi's service, Otani Yoshitsugu shared with his friend Ishida Mitsunari the same rise through bureaucratic rather than military channels, which drew them close within the Toyotomi clan despite the illness, likely leprosy, that gradually weakened him.

In 1600, though he first tried to dissuade Mitsunari from defying Tokugawa Ieyasu, judging the venture doomed, he ultimately chose to join him out of personal loyalty rather than calculation. At Sekigahara, too weak to fight on horseback, he directed his troops from a litter and took his own life after watching Kobayakawa Hideaki's defection decide the battle against his side.
