A renowned strategist, Kuroda Kanbei (also known as Yoshitaka, and later as Josui after taking Buddhist vows) became one of Toyotomi Hideyoshi's most trusted military advisers, helping plan numerous campaigns, including the 1590 siege of Odawara.

In 1600, while his son Nagamasa fought alongside Tokugawa Ieyasu at Sekigahara, Kanbei raised his own army on the island of Kyushu and led a lightning campaign there, taking advantage of the chaos, perhaps for a time harbouring ambitions of his own. Ieyasu's swift victory, however, cut short these designs before they could fully materialise.
