A niece of the daimyo Imagawa Yoshimoto, Tsukiyama-dono married the young Matsudaira Motoyasu, the future Tokugawa Ieyasu, in 1557, while he was still a vassal of the Imagawa clan; the marriage, arranged by Yoshimoto himself, was meant to tighten ties between the two houses. She bore him a son, Matsudaira Nobuyasu, and a daughter, Kamehime.

After Ieyasu broke with the Imagawa to ally with Oda Nobunaga, her Imagawa origins cast growing suspicion on her. In 1579, accused along with her son Nobuyasu of secretly conspiring with the Takeda clan, she was executed on her own husband's order, under pressure, tradition holds, from Nobunaga himself; the truth of the accusation remains debated by historians today.
