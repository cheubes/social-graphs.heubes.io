One of Oda Nobunaga's oldest and most loyal generals, Shibata Katsuie had served the clan since the previous generation and was entrusted with the conquest and later governance of Echizen Province, where he fought notably against the Uesugi clan.

After Nobunaga's death in 1582, he opposed Toyotomi Hideyoshi at the succession council, favouring a different heir; the disagreement escalated into open conflict. Defeated at the Battle of Shizugatake in 1583, he retreated to his castle at Kitanosho where, surrounded by Hideyoshi's troops, he took his own life alongside his new wife Oichi rather than surrender.
