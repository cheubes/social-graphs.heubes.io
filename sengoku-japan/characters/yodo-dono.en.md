Born Chacha, the eldest daughter of Oichi and the daimyo Azai Nagamasa, she lost her father as a child when Oda Nobunaga crushed the Azai clan, and later her stepfather Shibata Katsuie and her mother, who took their own lives together in 1583 after their defeat by Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

Taken in by Hideyoshi, who became both her protector and, later, her lover, she bore him a son, Hideyori, in 1593, the regent's only surviving heir. Now known as Yodo-dono, after Hideyoshi's death in 1598 she wielded considerable influence as the young heir's mother and protector, standing with him against the rising power of Tokugawa Ieyasu.

During the sieges of Osaka Castle in 1614-1615, she co-led the last armed resistance to Tokugawa authority alongside her son. When the castle fell in June 1615, she took her own life together with Hideyori, bringing the Toyotomi line to a final end.
