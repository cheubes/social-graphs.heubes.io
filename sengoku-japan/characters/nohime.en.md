The daughter of Saito Dosan, daimyo of Mino Province, Nōhime married Oda Nobunaga around 1549 as part of an alliance meant to ease tensions between the Oda and Saito clans. The marriage produced no known heir, and contemporary sources say little about her, leaving her largely in the shadows of the chronicles.

Her trail fades after the mid-1570s: some later accounts claim she died young, others have her surviving until 1612, retired after Nobunaga's death. This documentary uncertainty, common for the wives of secondary-ranked daimyo, has not stopped later tradition from casting her as a political adviser to her husband, though no contemporary source directly confirms it.
