The second son of Mori Motonari, adopted into the Kikkawa house to lead it, he became one of the two military pillars of his father's 'Two Rivers' system, alongside his brother Kobayakawa Takakage.

Fierce and uncompromising, he resisted any form of accommodation with Oda Nobunaga and later Toyotomi Hideyoshi far more stubbornly than his brother, embodying the most combative wing of the Mori clan in the face of advancing unification, before dying shortly before Hideyoshi's invasion of Kyushu.
