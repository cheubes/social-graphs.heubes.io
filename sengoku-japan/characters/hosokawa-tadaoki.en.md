A cultured daimyo recognised as a master of the tea ceremony, Hosokawa Tadaoki served first Oda Nobunaga and then Toyotomi Hideyoshi before marrying Tama, the daughter of Akechi Mitsuhide, in 1578; she would later become known as Hosokawa Gracia.

When his father-in-law betrayed and assassinated Nobunaga in 1582, Tadaoki refused to follow him and remained loyal to Nobunaga's cause and later Hideyoshi's, at the cost of a painful rift with his wife, whom he kept secluded for several years out of political caution. In 1600, he sided firmly with Tokugawa Ieyasu; Gracia's death during the campaign, targeted by Ishida Mitsunari, only strengthened his resolve to fight at Sekigahara, where he distinguished himself.
