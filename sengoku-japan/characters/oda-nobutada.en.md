The eldest son and designated heir of Oda Nobunaga, he was given command of military campaigns from an early age, notably against the Takeda clan, which he helped destroy in 1582. Nobunaga formally handed him leadership of the Oda clan during his own lifetime, an unusual arrangement meant to secure a smooth succession.

On the very day Akechi Mitsuhide surrounded his father at the Honno-ji temple, Nobutada was nearby at Nijo Castle. Rather than flee, he chose to resist the attackers before taking his own life, depriving the Oda clan of both its leaders in a single day and opening the succession struggle that Toyotomi Hideyoshi would go on to win.
