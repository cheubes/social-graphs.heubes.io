The eldest son and designated heir of Tokugawa Ieyasu, Matsudaira Nobuyasu married a daughter of Oda Nobunaga and commanded troops from his teenage years during the campaigns against the Takeda clan, where he distinguished himself through his bravery.

In 1579, he was accused, along with his mother Tsukiyama-dono, of secretly communicating with the enemy Takeda. Under pressure from Nobunaga, an ally whose support Ieyasu could not afford to lose, he was forced into seppuku at the age of twenty, a painful episode that left the Tokugawa clan without a direct heir for a time.
