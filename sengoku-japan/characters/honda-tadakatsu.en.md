A vassal of Tokugawa Ieyasu since childhood, Honda Tadakatsu fought at his side in every major battle of the clan for more than fifty years, from the defeat at Mikatagahara against Takeda Shingen to Sekigahara, never seriously wounded in any of them, a reputation for invulnerability reinforced by his emblematic antlered helmet.

Regarded as one of the Tokugawa's 'Four Guardian Kings' alongside Ii Naomasa, Sakai Tadatsugu and Sakakibara Yasumasa, he was praised even by his enemies for his bravery, to the point that Takeda Shingen is said to have once called him 'a treasure surpassing even Ieyasu's own.'
