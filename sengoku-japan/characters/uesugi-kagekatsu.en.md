The nephew and adopted son of Uesugi Kenshin, Kagekatsu took over the Uesugi clan after a brief succession war following Kenshin's death in 1578, then aligned with Toyotomi Hideyoshi, who made him one of his five great regents.

After Hideyoshi's death, his relationship with Tokugawa Ieyasu deteriorated quickly: accused of fortifying his domain and preparing a revolt, he refused to justify himself in person before Ieyasu, prompting Ieyasu to launch the Aizu campaign against him in 1600. It was this march north that left the field open for Ishida Mitsunari to raise the Western coalition, indirectly making Kagekatsu one of the triggers of the Sekigahara campaign.
