A long-serving general of Oda Nobunaga, known for his quiet loyalty and administrative efficiency, Niwa Nagahide took part in most of the clan's major campaigns, from the building of Azuchi Castle to the fight against the Ikko-ikki.

After Nobunaga's assassination, he chose to back Toyotomi Hideyoshi in the succession struggle against Shibata Katsuie, providing decisive support to the victory at Shizugatake in 1583. This renewed loyalty allowed him to keep his fiefs until his natural death two years later.
