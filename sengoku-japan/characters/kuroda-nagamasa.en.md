The son of Kuroda Kanbei, raised partly as a hostage at Oda Nobunaga's court and later trained alongside Toyotomi Hideyoshi, Kuroda Nagamasa actively took part in the unification campaigns and the invasions of Korea, commanding in the field alongside Kato Kiyomasa.

After Hideyoshi's death, he drew closer to Tokugawa Ieyasu, even playing an active role in winning other lords over to his cause before Sekigahara. His decisive contribution to the 1600 victory, particularly in the negotiations preceding Kobayakawa Hideaki's defection, earned him rich rewards from the new Tokugawa regime.
