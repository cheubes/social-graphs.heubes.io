Born Akechi Tama, the daughter of Akechi Mitsuhide, she married the daimyo Hosokawa Tadaoki in 1578. Her life changed in 1582 when her father assassinated Oda Nobunaga: out of political caution, her husband kept her secluded for several years rather than risk being associated with the betrayal.

Secretly converted to Christianity around 1587 under the baptismal name Gracia, despite Hideyoshi's ban on samurai conversions, she thereafter led a quiet but firmly held life of faith. In 1600, when Ishida Mitsunari tried to take her hostage to force her husband to join the Western coalition, she refused to surrender and, her faith forbidding ritual suicide, asked a retainer to kill her rather than be captured, an episode that struck both sides deeply on the eve of Sekigahara.
