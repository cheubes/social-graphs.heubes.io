Nicknamed the 'One-Eyed Dragon' after losing an eye in childhood, likely to smallpox, Date Masamune took over the Date clan in northern Japan at the age of seventeen and immediately set about expanding his territory through a series of aggressive conquests against his neighbours.

His expansion ran into Toyotomi Hideyoshi's ultimatum in 1590, who demanded his submission during the siege of Odawara; Masamune arrived at the last moment, narrowly avoiding his clan's destruction, and was forced to give up part of his conquests. After Hideyoshi's death, he drew closer to Tokugawa Ieyasu, becoming a loyal ally while also pursuing, on the side, an ambitious and personal foreign policy, going so far as to send an embassy all the way to Europe.
