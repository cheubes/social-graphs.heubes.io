Entering Oda Nobunaga's service as a page before becoming one of his generals, Maeda Toshiie forged a lasting friendship over the course of the campaigns with Toyotomi Hideyoshi, then a lowly sandal-bearer destined for a very different future.

After Nobunaga's death, he backed Hideyoshi in the succession struggle and became one of his closest and wealthiest vassals, governing Kaga Province. His loyalty and prestige earned him appointment, shortly before Hideyoshi's death in 1598, as one of the five great regents entrusted with watching over the young Hideyori; he died, however, the very next year, depriving the Toyotomi clan of a counterweight capable of standing up to Tokugawa Ieyasu.
