The eldest of the Tokugawa's 'Four Guardian Kings,' Sakai Tadatsugu served Tokugawa Ieyasu from his earliest youth and became one of his most experienced commanders, playing a key role at the Battle of Nagashino in 1575 through a decisive flanking manoeuvre against the Takeda positions.

As much a diplomat as a soldier, he repeatedly represented Ieyasu before Oda Nobunaga and later Toyotomi Hideyoshi, helping preserve the alliances on which the Tokugawa clan's later rise would depend.
