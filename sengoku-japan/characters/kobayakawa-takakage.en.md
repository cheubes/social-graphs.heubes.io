The third son of Mori Motonari, adopted into the Kobayakawa house, he formed the 'Two Rivers' system with his brother Kikkawa Motoharu that militarily supported the Mori clan, distinguishing himself through a more flexible political sense than his brother's.

In 1582, while defending Takamatsu Castle under siege by Toyotomi Hideyoshi, news of Oda Nobunaga's assassination at Honno-ji precipitated a swift truce between the two sides; Takakage skilfully negotiated the agreement, which opened the way to closer ties between the Mori clan and Hideyoshi. Having become one of Hideyoshi's loyal supporters, he in turn adopted a son into the Kobayakawa house, Hideaki, who was destined for a very different fate.
