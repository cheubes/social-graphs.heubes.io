The younger sister of Oda Nobunaga, known for her beauty, Oichi was married in 1564 to the daimyo Azai Nagamasa to seal the alliance between the Oda and Azai clans. She bore him three daughters, including Yodo-dono, before her brother and husband turned on each other: when Nagamasa broke the alliance in 1570, Nobunaga eventually crushed the Azai clan in 1573, killing Nagamasa and leaving Oichi a widow with her daughters.

After Nobunaga's assassination in 1582, Oichi remarried to Shibata Katsuie, one of her brother's most loyal generals, then locked in a power struggle with Toyotomi Hideyoshi. Defeated at Shizugatake in 1583, the new union ended that same year in a joint suicide at besieged Kitanosho Castle: Oichi chose to die alongside her second husband rather than outlive him.
