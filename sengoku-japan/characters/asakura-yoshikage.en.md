The leader of the Asakura clan, lord of Echizen Province, Asakura Yoshikage briefly hosted the deposed shogun Ashikaga Yoshiaki before the latter chose to turn instead to Oda Nobunaga, seen as better placed to restore him to Kyoto.

His alliance with Azai Nagamasa allowed him to catch Nobunaga off guard in 1570, but the coalition met determined resistance at the Battle of Anegawa. Weakened by years of attrition, the Asakura clan was finally destroyed by Nobunaga in 1573; surrounded, Yoshikage took his own life shortly after the fall of the Azai clan, his last ally.
