The youngest of the Tokugawa's 'Four Guardian Kings,' Sakakibara Yasumasa distinguished himself as early as the Battle of Mikatagahara in 1572 and became one of Tokugawa Ieyasu's trusted commanders, notably during the conflict against Toyotomi Hideyoshi at Komaki-Nagakute in 1584.

At the Battle of Sekigahara in 1600, he was tasked with escorting and protecting Tokugawa Hidetada, Ieyasu's son, who was delayed en route by the siege of Ueda Castle held by the Sanada clan, preventing him from arriving in time to take part in the main fighting.
