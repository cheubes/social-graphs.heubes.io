The daimyo of Omi Province, Azai Nagamasa sealed an alliance with Oda Nobunaga in 1564 by marrying his sister Oichi, an alliance that secured Nobunaga a strategic route toward Kyoto.

In 1570, forced to choose between his long-standing loyalty to the Asakura clan and his alliance with Nobunaga, he broke with the latter and joined the Asakura, catching Nobunaga off guard during his campaign against them. This betrayal, heavy with consequences for Oichi and their daughters, ended in the destruction of the Azai clan in 1573: surrounded in his castle at Odani, Nagamasa took his own life.
