Orphaned as a young child, he entered the Carmelite convent of Santa Maria del Carmine in Florence while still a boy, where he could study Masaccio's frescoes in the Brancacci Chapel.

Protected by Cosimo de' Medici despite a turbulent life - he eloped with the nun Lucrezia Buti, mother of his son Filippino -, he executed fresco cycles in the cathedrals of Prato and Spoleto for his Florentine patrons.

Sandro Botticelli trained in his workshop, inheriting his sense of line and grace. Fra Filippo Lippi died in Spoleto in 1469, while still at work on the cathedral's decoration.
