Born into a Milanese family of painters and miniaturists, he entered the service of the Sforza court as a portraitist: a portrait of Bianca Maria Sforza, betrothed and then wife of the future Emperor Maximilian I, is attributed to him. Around 1483 he joined forces with Leonardo da Vinci to paint The Virgin of the Rocks for a Milanese confraternity, a commission whose final settlement sparked a dispute that dragged on for more than twenty years. His exact birthplace and birth date remain uncertain, put at around 1455, and he disappears from the record after 1508, the last documented trace of his activity. What survives of his work nonetheless makes him one of the few direct witnesses to Leonardo's years in Milan.
