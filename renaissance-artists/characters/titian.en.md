Born around 1488 in Pieve di Cadore, he trained in Venice under Giovanni Bellini, where he worked alongside Giorgione, joining him in 1508 on the now-lost façade frescoes of the Fondaco dei Tedeschi.

On Bellini's death in 1516 he became official painter to the Republic and worked for Alfonso I d'Este, who commissioned from him a mythological series for his camerino d'alabastro in Ferrara. His intimate friend Pietro Aretino helped spread his fame across Europe from Venice, while a stay in Rome in 1545-1546 brought him face to face with Michelangelo.

He died in Venice in 1576, carried off by an outbreak of plague.
