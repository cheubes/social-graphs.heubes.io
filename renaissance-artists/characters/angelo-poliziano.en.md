Born in 1454 in Montepulciano, which gave him his name, he lost his father young, moved to Florence, and studied Greek there under John Argyropoulos before befriending Marsilio Ficino. Taken into the Medici household, he became tutor to Lorenzo de' Medici's children and wrote major works such as the Stanze per la giostra and the dramatic fable Orfeo. Present in the cathedral during the Pazzi conspiracy of 1478, he helped Lorenzo take refuge in the sacristy while Giuliano, Lorenzo's brother, was murdered. He died in 1494, the same year as his friend Pico della Mirandola, with whom he was buried at the San Marco convent.
