The daughter of Ercole I d'Este and Isabella d'Este's younger sister, she was betrothed as a child to Ludovico Sforza, regent and later Duke of Milan, whom she married in 1491. At the Milanese court, where Leonardo da Vinci was then in her husband's service, she emerged as an active political figure, travelling on a diplomatic mission to Venice in 1493 to strengthen Ludovico's alliance with the Republic. Fond of festivities and fine dress, she helped make Milan one of the most brilliant artistic centres of northern Italy. She died in January 1497, aged twenty-one, from complications of childbirth, leaving Ludovico Sforza inconsolable.
