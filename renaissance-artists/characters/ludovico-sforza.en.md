The son of Francesco Sforza and known as "il Moro", he governed the Duchy of Milan as regent for his nephew Gian Galeazzo Sforza from 1480, before becoming duke in his own right in 1494. Married to Beatrice d'Este in 1491, he drew Leonardo da Vinci to his court, where the artist painted The Last Supper and worked on the unfinished project for a monumental bronze horse honouring his father. In 1494, his appeal to King Charles VIII of France to back his ambitions over the Kingdom of Naples opened the way to the Italian Wars, which ultimately turned against him. Driven out of Milan by Louis XII, he was captured in 1500 and died in captivity in France in 1508.
