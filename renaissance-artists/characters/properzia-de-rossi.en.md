Born in Bologna in 1490, she first attracted attention for miniature reliefs carved into fruit stones, a virtuoso exercise that revealed her technical skill early on. She later turned to monumental marble sculpture and, in the 1520s, contributed to the portal reliefs of the Basilica of San Petronio in Bologna alongside the sculptor Alfonso Lombardi. This recognition, rare for a woman in the field, made her the only female sculptor Giorgio Vasari included among the artists of his time in his Lives. She died in 1530, a few years after working on that project, which remains her best-documented achievement.
