The daughter of Ercole I d'Este, Duke of Ferrara, and sister of Alfonso I d'Este and Beatrice d'Este, she married Francesco II Gonzaga, Marquess of Mantua, in 1490, and acted as regent of the marquisate when Venice took him prisoner during the Italian Wars. An insatiable collector, she filled the studiolo of the ducal palace with works commissioned from Perugino and Mantegna, whom she retained in her service, while running a vast network of correspondents who sought out antiquities and paintings for her across Italy. Remaining close to her brother Alfonso, with whom she stayed politically allied, she died in Mantua in 1539, after nearly half a century at the Mantuan court.
