The son of the painter Jacopo Bellini and brother of Gentile Bellini, he began in the family workshop by following the compositions of his brother-in-law Andrea Mantegna, before developing a light and colour sense of his own.

Appointed official painter to the Venetian Republic in 1483, he painted major altarpieces, such as the one for San Zaccaria, using oil paint to achieve new atmospheric effects.

Giorgione and Titian trained in his workshop, while Vittore Carpaccio worked in his orbit. He died in Venice in 1516, still active, having shaped an entire generation of Venetian painters.
