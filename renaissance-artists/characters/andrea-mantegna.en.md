Trained in Padua in the workshop of Francesco Squarcione, he married Nicolosia Bellini, sister of the painter Giovanni Bellini, who initially followed his compositions before developing a colour sense of his own.

Called to the court of Mantua in 1460, he became the Gonzaga family's official painter for more than forty years, producing for their ducal palace the Camera degli Sposi with its celebrated illusionistic oculus, as well as the great cycle of the Triumphs of Caesar. Isabella d'Este, who arrived in Mantua by marriage, made him the official painter of her studiolo, for which he painted Parnassus.

He died in Mantua in 1506, after more than four decades in the service of the same dynasty.
