Active in Bologna in the early sixteenth century, he specialised in sculpted portraiture in terracotta and wax, a genre in which his skill earned him a strong reputation across Emilia. In the 1520s he collaborated with the sculptor Properzia de' Rossi on the portal reliefs of the Basilica of San Petronio, one of the city's great decorative projects. That reputation drew the attention of Alfonso I d'Este, who brought him to the Ferrara court, where he became one of the duke's official sculptors. He died in 1537, having divided his career between the two cities.
