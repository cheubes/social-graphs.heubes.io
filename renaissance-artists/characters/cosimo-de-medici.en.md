Born in 1389, he and his brother inherited the family bank founded by their father, Giovanni di Bicci, and built it into the most powerful in Europe. Briefly exiled by the rival Albizzi family in 1433, he returned in triumph the following year and ruled Florence from behind the scenes for the rest of his life, without ever holding a permanent official post.

A tireless patron, he funded the rebuilding of the San Marco convent where Fra Angelico painted, backed several of Filippo Brunelleschi's building projects, made Michelozzo his house architect, and remained close to Donatello.

At his death in 1464, Florence posthumously granted him the title pater patriae, and his grandson Lorenzo carried his legacy forward.
