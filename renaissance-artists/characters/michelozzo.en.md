Trained as a sculptor, he partnered with Donatello for over a decade in a shared workshop, producing several tombs together before turning to architecture.

He accompanied Cosimo de' Medici into exile in Venice in 1433-1434, then became the family's house architect on their return. He built Cosimo's Florentine palace, now the Palazzo Medici Riccardi, and remodelled the convent of San Marco for him, fitting its library with an elegant colonnaded hall.

Michelozzo died in Florence in 1472, leaving a lasting architectural mark on the city.
