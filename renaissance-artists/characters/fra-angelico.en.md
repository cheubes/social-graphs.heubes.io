He entered the Dominican order at the convent of Fiesole under the name Fra Giovanni, and absorbed from Masaccio a sense of volume and space that he put to the service of devotional painting.

Cosimo de' Medici funded the rebuilding of the convent of San Marco in Florence, where he painted, for the friars' cells, a vast fresco cycle conceived as aids to meditation. In his workshop he trained the young Benozzo Gozzoli, who assisted him before developing his own colourful, narrative style.

Summoned to Rome to decorate chapels in the Vatican, Fra Angelico died there in 1455.
