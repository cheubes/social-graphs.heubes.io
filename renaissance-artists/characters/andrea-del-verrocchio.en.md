Trained as a goldsmith before establishing himself as a sculptor and painter, he ran one of the busiest workshops in Florence, where he produced his bronze David and, for the Medici, the tomb of Piero and Giovanni de' Medici in San Lorenzo.

Leonardo da Vinci trained there and collaborated on his Baptism of Christ, painting one of the angels - a result said to have led Verrocchio to give up painting altogether.

Summoned to Venice to cast the equestrian statue of the condottiero Bartolomeo Colleoni, he died in 1488 without seeing the bronze completed, a task finished by Alessandro Leopardi.
