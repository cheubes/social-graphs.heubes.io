Born probably around 1477 in Castelfranco Veneto, he trained, like Titian, in the workshop of Giovanni Bellini, then the official painter of the Venetian Republic.

He went on to develop an enigmatic style of painting in which landscape carries as much weight as the figures, as in The Tempest or the Sleeping Venus, left unfinished at his death. In 1508 he worked alongside the younger Titian on the now-lost façade frescoes of the Fondaco dei Tedeschi in Venice.

He died in 1510, probably of the plague, leaving a small and disputed body of work whose influence shaped Venetian painting for decades, Titian's above all.
