Born in 1422, he became one of the most sought-after condottieri in Italy, hiring out his military skills to the peninsula's great powers to finance his court at Urbino. Disfigured in the right eye during a jousting accident, he had that profile immortalised in the famous double portrait diptych Piero della Francesca painted of him and his wife, Battista Sforza.

In 1474, Pope Sixtus IV raised him to the rank of Duke of Urbino, sealing a close alliance between the two men. A keen bibliophile, he turned his palace into a renowned centre of art and learning until his death in 1482.
