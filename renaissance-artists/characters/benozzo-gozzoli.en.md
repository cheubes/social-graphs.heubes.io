A goldsmith's apprentice in Lorenzo Ghiberti's workshop, where he worked on the doors of the Florence Baptistery, he then turned to painting as an assistant to Fra Angelico, accompanying him to Rome and Orvieto among other places. He developed a colourful, narrative style of his own, displayed to great effect in the Procession of the Magi painted for the Medici palace chapel, which includes portraits of his patrons. The later decades of his career took him to San Gimignano and then Pisa, where he covered the walls of the Camposanto with a vast cycle of Old Testament frescoes. He died in Pisa in 1497.
