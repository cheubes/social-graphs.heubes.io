Born in 1446 in Città della Pieve, in Umbria, he trained in Verrocchio's Florentine workshop alongside the young Leonardo da Vinci. Summoned to Rome by Pope Sixtus IV, he led the team of painters assembled in 1481-1482 to decorate the walls of the Sistine Chapel, alongside Sandro Botticelli, Domenico Ghirlandaio and Luca Signorelli, painting among other scenes the Delivery of the Keys. His clear, balanced style won him a prestigious clientele, including Isabella d'Este, and left a lasting mark on his pupil Raphael, who trained in his workshop. He died in 1523 at Fontignano, near Perugia, his reputation by then diminished by the harsher judgment of the following generation.
