Trained as an architect before turning to painting, he joins the Cubist circle and exhibits at the 1911 Salon des Indépendants alongside Robert Delaunay and Juan Gris, within the group that becomes known as the Section d'Or.

Marked by his experience of the First World War, he develops a painting style of tubular, mechanical forms celebrating the industrial world and modern life, which he extends to film with Ballet mécanique in 1924.
