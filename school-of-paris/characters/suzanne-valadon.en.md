A former circus acrobat turned model for Renoir, Puvis de Chavannes and Toulouse-Lautrec, she studies their technique and teaches herself to draw and paint, eventually becoming the first woman admitted to the Société Nationale des Beaux-Arts.

The mother of Maurice Utrillo, whose vocation she encourages, she paints female nudes with a frankness unusual for a woman of her time, alongside portraits and still lifes, in a vigorous, brightly coloured style that sets her apart from her contemporaries.
