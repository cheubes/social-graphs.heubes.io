The son of a German banker destined for a career in finance, he chooses the art trade instead and opens his own Paris gallery in 1907, where he meets Picasso and becomes the principal dealer of early Cubism, tying exclusive contracts with Picasso, Braque, Gris and Léger.

A German national, he is forced into exile in Switzerland during the First World War, his stock then seized and auctioned off by the French state. He rebuilds a new gallery after the war and remains, until his death in 1979, one of the great theorists and champions of Cubism.
