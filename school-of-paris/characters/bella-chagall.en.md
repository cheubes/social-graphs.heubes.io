The daughter of a wealthy jewellery-trading family in Vitebsk, she meets Marc Chagall in 1909 and becomes his constant muse, present throughout much of his painted work, before marrying him in 1915.

She accompanies him through all his exiles, from revolutionary Russia to France and then the United States in 1941. A writer herself, she pens memoirs in Yiddish about her childhood in Vitebsk, published after her death. She dies of an infection in 1944, in the midst of the war, leaving Chagall inconsolable.
