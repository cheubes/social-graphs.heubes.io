A Polish poet who comes to Paris to study literature, he turns to art dealing for lack of sufficient income and devotes himself to championing three artists he admires: Modigliani, Soutine and Kisling.

He provides Modigliani with lodging, a studio and a regular income until his death in 1920, organising in 1917 his only solo exhibition, which is shut down by the police. Ruined by the 1929 crash after spending his fortune on his artists, he dies in poverty in 1932.
