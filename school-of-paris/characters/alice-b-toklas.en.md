Born in San Francisco, she settles in Paris in 1907 and meets Gertrude Stein soon after her arrival, moving in with her shortly afterward and becoming her companion for the rest of her life.

She runs the couple's household and receives, alongside Stein, the artists and writers passing through Rue de Fleurus, keeping conversation with the wives and companions of guests just as Stein does with the artists themselves. After Stein's death in 1946, she publishes several books, including her own memoirs, and looks after her legacy until her own death in 1967.
