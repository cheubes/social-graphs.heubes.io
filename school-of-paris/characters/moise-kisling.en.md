Trained at the Kraków School of Fine Arts, he settles in Paris in 1910 and becomes an unmissable social figure of Montparnasse, a close friend of Modigliani with whom he shares a studio for a time.

His painting, made up of elegant portraits and stylised, brightly coloured nudes, achieves considerable commercial success during his lifetime. Enlisting in the Foreign Legion and then exiled to the United States during the Second World War as a Jew, he returns to settle in France after the war.
