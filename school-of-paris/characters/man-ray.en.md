An American painter first active in the Dada movement in New York, he settles in Paris in 1921 on Marcel Duchamp's advice and turns to photography, which quickly becomes his main livelihood as a portraitist of Montparnasse artists and writers.

Kiki de Montparnasse's companion from 1921 to 1929, he creates with her some of his most famous images, including Le Violon d'Ingres, and develops with the photographer Lee Miller the solarisation technique. A figure of early Surrealism, he remains throughout his life attached to painting, which he considers his true art.
