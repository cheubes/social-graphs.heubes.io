A poet born in Rome to a Polish mother, he settles in Paris and becomes the principal theorist and champion of Cubism, publicising it through his articles and his essay The Cubist Painters (1913), and forms a close friendship with Picasso and many Montparnasse artists.

He himself coins the term "Surrealism" shortly before his death. Volunteering for the army and wounded in the head in 1916, he marries Jacqueline Kolb a few months before dying of the Spanish flu, two days before the 1918 armistice.
