Born Maria Vorobieff in the Volga region, she studies art in Moscow and then Italy before settling in Paris in 1912, where the writer Maxim Gorky gives her the nickname "Marevna", which she keeps for life.

Part of the Montparnasse Cubist circle, she has a turbulent relationship with the painter Diego Rivera, with whom she has a daughter. A painter herself, working in a personal Cubist idiom, she also leaves valuable memoirs of the Montparnasse bohemia she moved through, published as Life in Two Worlds.
