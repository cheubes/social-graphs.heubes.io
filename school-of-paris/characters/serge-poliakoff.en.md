From a wealthy Moscow family scattered by the 1917 revolution, he wanders across Europe as a cabaret guitarist before settling in Paris in 1923 and taking up painting relatively late, in the 1930s.

Close to Sonia and Robert Delaunay, who introduce him to abstraction, he develops after the war a personal style of abstract compositions with simple forms and dense colours, aligning him with the later generation of the School of Paris associated with lyrical abstraction.
