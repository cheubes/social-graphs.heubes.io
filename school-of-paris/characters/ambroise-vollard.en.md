Born in Réunion, he settles in Paris to study law before turning to the picture trade and opening his own gallery in 1893, where he exhibits Cézanne early on, then the artists of the following generation.

In 1901 he organises the first Paris exhibition of a young, still-unknown Pablo Picasso, and later supports Derain and Vlaminck in their early Fauvist years. A publisher of art books as much as a dealer, he dies in 1939 in a car accident.
