Born in Ukraine, she is raised by a wealthy uncle in Saint Petersburg who funds her art studies in Germany and then Paris, where she meets and, in 1910, marries the painter Robert Delaunay.

Together they develop Orphism, an art of pure colour she applies equally to painting, textiles, fashion and decoration, blurring the boundary between art and craft. She continues painting and exhibiting long after her husband's death, until her own passing in 1979.
