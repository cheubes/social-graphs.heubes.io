Trained at the Kyiv School of Art, he settles in Paris in 1908, frequenting La Ruche and then the Cubist circle, and develops an innovative sculpture that introduces void as a compositional element in its own right.

He exhibits at the Salon des Indépendants and with the Section d'Or alongside Cubist painters from 1911-1912 onward. Emigrating to the United States in 1923, he pursues a recognised career as a sculptor and teacher there until his death in New York in 1964.
