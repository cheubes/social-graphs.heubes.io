Born in Ukraine to a Jewish family that emigrates to Ottoman Palestine during her childhood, she moves alone to Paris in 1910 to study dressmaking before turning to sculpture at the École des Beaux-Arts.

She becomes a sought-after portrait sculptor, producing busts of numerous Montparnasse figures including Modigliani, who also paints her portrait. Taking refuge in Switzerland during the Occupation as a Jew, she returns after the war to a recognised career until her death in 1968.
