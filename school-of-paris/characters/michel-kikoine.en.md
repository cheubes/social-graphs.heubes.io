A childhood friend of Soutine and Krémègne in Minsk, he studies with them in Vilnius before joining them in Paris in 1912 and settling, like them, at La Ruche, forming with his two compatriots a trio that stays close for life.

An expressionist painter of landscapes, still lifes and nudes, he develops a body of work in muted colours shaped by the same influences as his friends, without ever achieving their renown. Naturalised French, he continues painting until his death in 1968.
