A student at the Académie Colarossi, she meets Modigliani in 1917 and becomes his companion and favoured model, painted by him in a long series of portraits that ranks among his most celebrated work.

A painter herself, her own work remains overshadowed by her companion's and by the tragic circumstances of her death: pregnant with their second child, she takes her own life the day after Modigliani's death, in January 1920.
