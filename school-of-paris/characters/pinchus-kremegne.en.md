Born in a small town in present-day Belarus, he studies sculpture in Vilnius alongside Kikoïne and Soutine before joining them in Paris in 1912, settling at La Ruche and turning to painting.

An expressionist painter of landscapes and still lifes, he remains in the shadow of his friends Soutine and Chagall despite a prolific career spanning nearly seven decades. He continues painting from his home in Céret, in the Pyrenees, until his death in 1981.
