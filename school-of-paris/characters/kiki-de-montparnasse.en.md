Born Alice Prin into a very modest family, she becomes a model for numerous Montparnasse artists from her teenage years onward, including Foujita, Kisling and Soutine, before performing as a cabaret singer under the name Kiki.

The muse and companion of the photographer Man Ray from 1921 to 1929, she poses for his most famous images, including Le Violon d'Ingres. A central figure of Montparnasse bohemian life, nicknamed "the Queen of Montparnasse", she publishes her memoirs as early as 1929, prefaced by Ernest Hemingway for the American edition.
