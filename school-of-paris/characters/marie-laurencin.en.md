A student at the Académie Humbert, she frequents the Bateau-Lavoir and grows close to the Cubist circle through her relationship with Guillaume Apollinaire, who presents her as a painter in her own right rather than a mere muse.

She develops a personal style of pastel tones and stylised female figures, far removed from the angular aesthetic of the Cubism practised around her. Exiled in Spain during the First World War after marrying a German painter, she goes on to a recognised career until her death.
