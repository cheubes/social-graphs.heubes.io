Close to early Cubism, he moves away from it to develop, together with his wife Sonia Delaunay, a pictorial language built on the contrast of pure colours, which the poet Apollinaire names "Orphism".

His Windows series, followed by the Circular Forms, explores colour as the painting's sole subject, a shared pursuit the couple continues in parallel until his death in 1941, after which Sonia carries their common work forward alone.
