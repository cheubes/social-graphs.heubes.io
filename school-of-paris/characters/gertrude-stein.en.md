An American writer settled in Paris from 1903 with her brother Leo, she opens a salon at 27 Rue de Fleurus where artists and writers cross paths, and becomes, together with her brother, one of the very earliest collectors of Picasso and Matisse.

Separating from her brother in 1913 amid disagreements, she continues alone in her role as patron, notably supporting Juan Gris during his dealer Kahnweiler's wartime exile. The partner of Alice B. Toklas for nearly four decades, she pursues in parallel an experimental literary body of work that influences the Lost Generation of American writers in Paris.
