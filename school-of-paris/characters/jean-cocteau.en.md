A poet, novelist, playwright and filmmaker who tried his hand at everything, he frequents Parisian artistic circles from his youth and befriends Picasso, whom he brings in 1917 to the ballet Parade, set to music by Erik Satie, whose scandal marks Cubism's debut on stage.

He remains throughout his life a go-between across the arts, bringing painters, musicians and writers together, while pursuing his own body of work blending poetry, theatre and film, from The Blood of a Poet to Beauty and the Beast.
