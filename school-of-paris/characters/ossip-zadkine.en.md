Born in Vitebsk like Chagall, he studies in London before settling in Paris in 1910, where he frequents La Ruche and befriends artists of the Cubist circle while developing his own sculpture, between expressionist figuration and African influences.

Naturalised French, he fights in the French army during the First World War and flees to the United States during the Second. He is best known for The Destroyed City (1953), a monument commemorating the German bombing of Rotterdam in 1940.
