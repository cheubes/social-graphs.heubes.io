title: "The School of Paris"
---

At the turn of the twentieth century, painters and sculptors from across Europe and beyond converge on the studios of Montparnasse, crossing paths with dealers, patrons and muses along the way. Out of this cosmopolitan ferment emerge Fauvism, Cubism and a bohemian scene that remains legendary.
