FBI Director since 1924, he viewed the civil rights movement, and Martin Luther King in particular, as infiltrated by communists and a threat to the American social order. He authorized the wiretapping of King and fueled a harassment campaign aimed at discrediting him.

The domestic counterintelligence program COINTELPRO, which he expanded in the mid-1960s, also targeted the Nation of Islam and the SNCC, part of a surveillance and disruption strategy that was only fully revealed after his death in 1972.
