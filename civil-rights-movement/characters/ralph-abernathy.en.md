A Montgomery minister, he was one of the organizers of the bus boycott alongside Martin Luther King, with whom he forged a lasting friendship and political partnership. He co-founded the SCLC with King in 1957 and remained his closest lieutenant throughout the following decade's campaigns.

Upon King's death in 1968, he succeeded him as SCLC president and carried forward the Poor People's Campaign King had launched, though he struggled to keep the organization at the same level of influence in the years that followed.
