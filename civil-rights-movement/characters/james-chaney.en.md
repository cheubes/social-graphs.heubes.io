A young Black man from Meridian, Mississippi, he joined CORE as a volunteer and helped organize Freedom Summer in 1964, the Black voter registration campaign jointly run by CORE and the SNCC under the COFO umbrella.

On June 21, 1964, he traveled with Michael Schwerner and Andrew Goodman to investigate the burning of a Black church in Neshoba County. Arrested and then released at night by a deputy sheriff, the three men were intercepted and killed by Ku Klux Klan members; their bodies were not found until six weeks later.
