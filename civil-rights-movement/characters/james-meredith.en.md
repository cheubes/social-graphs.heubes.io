A US Air Force veteran, he applied to the University of Mississippi in 1961 with NAACP backing. His admission, ordered by federal courts, triggered riots on the Oxford campus in 1962 that left two dead and forced President Kennedy to send federal troops to protect him and enforce the court order.

In 1966, he undertook a solo march across Mississippi to encourage Black voter registration and denounce the persistence of fear; shot by a gunman on the second day, he survived his wounds, and the march was taken up by the movement's major organizations, including Stokely Carmichael's SNCC.
