A full-fledged NAACP activist alongside her husband Medgar Evers, she spent the decades following his 1963 assassination seeking justice, after two early trials ended with all-white juries unable to reach a verdict.

Her persistence paid off in 1994, when a third trial finally convicted the killer. She went on to chair the NAACP, continuing the couple's political commitment.
