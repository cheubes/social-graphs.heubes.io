Attorney General under his brother President John F. Kennedy, he sent federal marshals to protect the Freedom Riders attacked in Alabama in 1961, while also authorizing, under pressure from J. Edgar Hoover, the wiretapping of Martin Luther King in the name of national security.

A senator and then a 1968 Democratic presidential candidate, he built a coalition with minorities and the poor, before being assassinated in Los Angeles that June, just two months after King.
