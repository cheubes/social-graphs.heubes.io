A student in Nashville, she led the sit-ins that desegregated the city's lunch counters in 1960 and became one of the founders of the SNCC. When the 1961 Freedom Rides were halted by violence in Birmingham, it was she who organized, against the advice of more cautious leaders, the relay of volunteers that allowed the buses to continue into Alabama.

She married James Bevel, with whom she planned several SCLC campaigns, including the 1965 Selma march.
