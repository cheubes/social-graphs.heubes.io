A journalist and president of the Arkansas NAACP branch, she took charge of organizing the integration of Little Rock Central High School in 1957, selecting and personally accompanying the nine Black students who faced hostile crowds and ultimately had to be protected by federal paratroopers sent by President Eisenhower.

Her own newspaper, which she ran with her husband, suffered a segregationist advertising boycott in retaliation and had to close a few years later.
