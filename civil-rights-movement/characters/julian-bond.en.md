A student at Atlanta's Morehouse College, he took part in founding the SNCC in 1960 and became its communications director, responsible for relaying the organization's actions to the national press. His media ease made him a natural spokesman for the student movement early on.

He went on to a long political career in Georgia, though the state legislature initially refused to seat him as a representative because of his opposition to the Vietnam War, before the Supreme Court ordered him reinstated.
