Birmingham's Commissioner of Public Safety for nearly a quarter century, he embodied the most brutal enforcement of segregation in a city nicknamed "Bombingham" for the frequency of unpunished racist bombings there.

In 1963, he ordered the use of high-pressure fire hoses and police dogs against peaceful demonstrators, including many children, during the campaign organized by the SCLC and Fred Shuttlesworth. Photographs of these scenes, broadcast around the world, turned public opinion in the movement's favor and hastened local negotiations.
