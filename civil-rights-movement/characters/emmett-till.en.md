A fourteen-year-old from Chicago, he was abducted, tortured and murdered in Mississippi in August 1955 after being accused of whistling at a white woman in a grocery store. His two killers were acquitted by an all-white jury, then confessed to the crime a few months later in a magazine interview, shielded from further prosecution.

His murder, and above all his mother's decision to display his mutilated body in an open casket, sent shockwaves across the nation widely credited as one of the sparks of the civil rights movement.
