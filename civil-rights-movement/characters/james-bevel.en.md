A minister trained at the American Baptist Theological Seminary alongside John Lewis, he became one of the SCLC's boldest strategists. It was his idea, controversial even within the movement, to involve children and high-schoolers in the 1963 Birmingham campaign, which magnified the media impact of the police violence.

He also conceived the 1965 Selma to Montgomery march in response to the killing of activist Jimmie Lee Jackson, and married SNCC activist Diane Nash, with whom he shared the planning of several campaigns.
