An attorney at the NAACP Legal Defense Fund led by Thurgood Marshall, she helped draft the briefs for Brown v. Board of Education and personally argued numerous school and university integration cases, including James Meredith's admission to the University of Mississippi in 1962.

The first African American woman to argue before the Supreme Court, she became in 1966 the first Black woman appointed as a federal judge in the United States.
