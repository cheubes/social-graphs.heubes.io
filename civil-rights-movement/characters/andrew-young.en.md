A minister and close adviser to Martin Luther King, he joined the SCLC in 1961 and became its executive director, playing a key role in negotiations with local authorities during the Birmingham and Selma campaigns. He was with King at the moment of his assassination in Memphis in 1968.

He went on to a prominent political career, becoming a US Congressman, US Ambassador to the United Nations, and then mayor of Atlanta.
